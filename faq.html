Questions

Does this work at my school?

If your school's LMS runs on D2L (often white-labeled under its own name — OnQ, LEARN, or something else entirely), yes. Enter your school's domain when you install it.

Do I need to log in to DUEonq separately?

No. It reads your existing, already-logged-in D2L session in your browser — there's no separate account to create.

Can my school see that I'm using this?

DUEonq makes the same kind of requests your browser already makes when you click around D2L. It doesn't identify itself as anything unusual, and it doesn't add, change, or submit anything on your behalf.

Is this an official university tool?

No. DUEonq is an independent, student-built project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any university, D2L, or Brightspace.

Does DUEonq store or share my data?

Everything stays in your browser's local storage. There's no server to send it to. See the privacy policy for the full breakdown.

What if my school uses a different LMS entirely?

DUEonq only works with D2L / Brightspace. It won't work on Canvas, Moodle, or Blackboard's own platform.
