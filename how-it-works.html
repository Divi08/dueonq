Add to Chrome

Set up once, in under a minute

No account to create. No password to hand over. Just point it at your school and go.

1

Install the extension

Add DUEonq to Chrome and open the side panel from the toolbar icon.

2

Point it at your school

Enter your school's D2L / Brightspace domain once. DUEonq only ever reads from that address.

3

You're already logged in

DUEonq uses your existing D2L session in your browser — no separate account, no new password.

Don't know your school's domain?

It's whatever's in the address bar when you're logged into your LMS.

It usually looks like courses.yourschool.edu or yourschool.brightspacecloud.com — whatever it's branded as on your campus (OnQ, LEARN, Blackboard-alikes rebranded on D2L, etc.), if it's built on D2L, DUEonq can read it.

That's really it.

One install, one domain, and your deadlines are sorted.
